How a visit works

No surprises

Four steps from booking to driving away. You approve the cost before any repair starts.

  1. Book or walk in

    Walk-ins are always welcome. Book online and your time is held right away, or call the shop.

  2. We diagnose

    We find the actual cause, starting with what the car's computer reports.

  3. You approve

    You get the price and what it covers. Nothing is repaired until you say yes.

  4. We fix it

    The work is done and checked, and we call you when the car is ready.

Good to know

Common questions

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are always welcome. If you want a time set aside for your car, book online or call (214) 256-6347.

How do I know my booking is confirmed?

Right after you book you see a confirmation with a reference number, and if you gave an email address we send one there too. Need to change a booking? Call the shop.

Do you work on my make and model?

Yes. We service and repair all makes and models, domestic and import. Imports such as Acura, Audi, BMW, Honda, Mazda, Mercedes-Benz, Nissan, Porsche and Volvo are a specialty.

When are you open?

Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The shop is at 2557 Glenda Ln #1&2, Dallas, TX 75229.

Do you service business vehicles?

Yes. Fleet maintenance is available with priority scheduling. Call to talk through what your vehicles need.
